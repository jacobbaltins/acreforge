AcreForge

A U.S.-wide rural-property planning prototype.

Current status

The site designer, Home Builder, map context, and browser-local project saves are available as a prototype. Projects stay in this browser unless you export or deliberately publish a project.

All available AcreForge features are free until further notice. There are no paid plans or payment options in the app. Optional external model providers may charge separately under their own terms.

AcreForge is a working name only. A comprehensive trademark search has not been completed, so the name is not cleared for commercial launch.

Read the prototype data & privacy notice. It describes local browser storage, map requests, public exports, and the unverified status of the server-side payment configuration.

The app's Settings → Feedback route offers a user-controlled draft with a blank email recipient and a copy option. No feedback inbox is configured; AcreForge does not receive or transmit responses.
